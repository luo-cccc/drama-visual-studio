# Atlas: Genre & Subgenre Aesthetic Anchors & Cliché Traps

For each of the 15 subgenres, this file defines:
1. **❌ Low-Aesthetic Traps (`Forbidden` candidates):** cheap page-game clutter, shapeless photo-studio gauze, or gritty peasant/bureaucrat downgrades that ruin the genre's visual appeal.
2. **⚡ Core Fantasy & Aesthetic Tension (Derivation starting point):** the specific power system, fate conflict, or social tension that drives high-aesthetic character design in this subgenre.
3. **🔩 Haute-Couture & Structural Question:** how the character's Dao, power, or identity translates into silhouette, layering, and luxury material physics.

## 1. Xianxia (仙侠 — 东方高定仙魔幻想)

**Core premise:** Xianxia is **transcendent Oriental high-fantasy couture** (aligned with top-tier 3DCG animation such as *Juan Siliang*, *Mortal's Journey*, *Xian Ni*). Characters are shaped by their **Dao, cultivation art (功法), spiritual root (灵根), and karmic tension** — never by mundane peasant labour or feudal civil-service bureaucracy.
