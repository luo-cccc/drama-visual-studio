# Atlas: Genre & Subgenre Cliché Blacklists & Physical Pressure Anchors

Pre-bundled genre aesthetic modules turn character design into a one-row lookup. This file provides the opposite: for each of the 15 subgenres, it defines:
1. **Training-Mean Traps (`Forbidden` candidates):** what an unguided model or lazy designer reaches for 90% of the time. **Any element in this column is banned by default** unless the user's explicit prompt demands it, and at least two must be copied into the blueprint's `IFV Forbidden` line ([../contract/gates.md](../contract/gates.md) §1).
2. **World Physical Pressure (Derivation starting point):** what the subgenre's environment, economy, or institution physically does to a human body and to cloth.
3. **Structural Grounding Question:** the specific construction question your blueprint must answer for this world.

## 1. Xianxia (仙侠)

| Subgenre | ❌ Training-Mean Traps (Banned by default — put in `IFV Forbidden`) | ⚡ World Physical Pressure (Derive from here) | 🔩 Structural Grounding Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sect-Ranked (宗门序列)** | floating anti-gravity silk ribbons; moon-white + pale-blue default; lotus hair crowns; "more flowing layers = higher cultivation"; generic jade pendant at waist | institutional uniformity vs personal attrition: standardized cloth issued by rank, knee/cuff wear from daily rites, rigid posture rules, cold mountain humidity | *How does the garment physically lock the body into protocol, and which exact seam or clasp records their specific duty inside the sect?* |
| **Rogue (散修)** | brown beggar rags; generic bamboo hat +酒葫芦; clean studio robe with one artful tear; random leather straps crossing the chest | zero institutional supply chain: every piece of cloth or armour was salvaged, traded, or modified from incompatible sources; weather proofing, hidden storage weight | *Which two pieces of this outfit come from completely different origins, and how did the character stitch or buckle them together to survive?* |
| **Clan-Born (世家血脉)** | gold filigree on every surface; symmetrical phoenix/starflower hairpins; pearl-white + crimson + tear-mole; bridal-style trailing gauze with no support | ancestral surveillance and dynastic burden: garments that are older than the wearer, heirloom metal too heavy for comfort, strict collar/waist restraint hiding physical cost | *What part of this garment was inherited rather than made for them, and where does that ancestral structure pinch, weigh down, or restrict their body?* |
| **Tribulation-Marked (渡劫/天罚)** | black-and-purple tattered villain robes; glowing lightning cracks; kintsugi gold veins on obsidian; floating shards; demonic red eyes | extreme thermal and barometric trauma: cloth carbonized or fused to metal hardware, insulated wrappings over nerve damage, asymmetrical balance from a survived strike | *Where did the heat or force physically enter and exit the garment, and how has the tailoring been splinted or re-bound afterward?* |
| **Demonic-Path (魔修/异道)** | red-and-black colour reflex; horns, skulls, bat wings, spikes as personality; gratuitous blood splatter; smoky aura | physiological extraction and resource scarcity: cultivation as an industrial or surgical cost on the practitioner's own body — tourniquet bindings, chemical/mineral stains, protective aprons or seals | *What bodily price does their method extract, and which functional binding, seal, or protective layer on the garment manages that leak or pain?* |
| **Beast-Blood (妖修/兽血)** | cute plush animal ears/tail pasted onto a human idol; random fur trim on collar; ragged caveman pelts; neon venom-green accents | anatomical containment tension: a human tailoring system struggling to accommodate non-human joint angles, body heat, molt, or bone density | *Where was the human garment deliberately cut open, reinforced, or left unlaced so it does not rip when their anatomy shifts or overheats?* |

## 3. Wuxia, Historical, Modern & Gothic

| Subgenre | ❌ Training-Mean Traps (Banned by default — put in `IFV Forbidden`) | ⚡ World Physical Pressure (Derive from here) | 🔩 Structural Grounding Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wuxia (武侠江湖)** | floor-dragging sleeves that would tangle a blade; shiny chrome armour; neon fabric; brand-new uncreased linen | kinetic clearance, mud/rain travel, weapon draw paths (even when the weapon is absent in the sheet): bound calves, tucked hems, sweat and road dust | *How are the cuffs, hem, and waist tied back to clear the hands and feet for sudden movement, and where has travel weather marked the cloth?* |
| **Republican / Modern Historical (民国/近代)** | over-embroidered nightclub qipao on every woman; spotless white trench coat on every man; modern idol hair | collision of two tailoring systems (traditional flat-cut silk/cotton vs Western darted wool tailoring) under wartime or industrial scarcity | *Where do the two eras collide on this body (e.g. a Western wool waistcoat over an unbleached traditional inner shirt), and what occupational wear shows on the cuffs?* |
| **Contemporary Couture (现代高定)** | logo streetwear; random asymmetrical cutouts with no structural line; red-carpet sequin gown | one radical architectural tailoring thesis pushed to its physical limit: bonded seams, extreme proportion cut, uncompromising material contrast | *What single tailoring seam or structural support creates the entire silhouette without relying on surface jewellery?* |
| **Cyber / Neo-Oriental (赛博新国风)** | glowing neon cyan/magenta circuit lines drawn on cloth; cyberpunk visor/goggles; random dangling USB cables; plastic bikini armour | cold industrial prosthetics or high-tension polymers colliding with warm, breathable traditional natural fibres; heat dissipation and cable routing | *Where does hard industrial hardware physically bolt into or compress the soft traditional fabric, and what is the thermal/functional reason for that junction?* |
| **Dark Fantasy / Gothic (暗黑哥特)** | skulls, roses, bat wings, spiderwebs, blood-red lace; generic vampire corset | liturgical architecture, mourning quarantine, wax, ash, iron reliquaries, heavy damp wool and stiffened linen | *What liturgical or protective function does this enclosure serve, and what physical weight (iron, wax, heavy velvet) holds the silhouette down?* |
