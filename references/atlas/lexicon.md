# Atlas: Unbundled Atomic Physical Lexicon & Colour Discipline

**Nothing in this file is bundled by genre, archetype, or character type.**

Use this lexicon during `engine/03` and `pipeline/design.md` to build physically specific English clauses after your design decisions are made.

---

## 1. The Four Material Light Behaviours

Every garment and accessory must state how light interacts with its surface. A character sheet must include **at least two, ideally three** of the four behaviours below, plus the **BJD finish layer** on the hero ornament:

| Light Behaviour | Optical physics | Unbundled material nouns (pick & combine freely) | Prompt phrasing mechanics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Broad soft specular** | light scatters across a microscopic weave, nap, or chalky matte surface with no hard mirror edge | raw tussah silk with visible slub, unbleached hemp, boiled wool felt, brushed suede, unglazed stoneware, dry matte crepe, sun-bleached sailcloth, oiled parchment | `matte [material] with a dry chalky hand and soft grazing falloff` / `heavy [material] absorbing the key light along its nap` |
| **2. Tight specular** | a tiny, razor-sharp hot highlight along a polished, wet, or metallic bevel | hand-hammered bronze, blackened tempered steel, wet volcanic obsidian, carved mutton-fat jade, polished horn, lacquered wood, mercury-gilded silver, glazed ceramic | ` catching a razor-thin specular line along the bevel` / `polished [material] throwing a tight hot highlight` |
| **3. Translucent** | light passes through and scatters inside the medium; underlying silhouette or colour bleeds through | crisp silk organza, smoke-dyed cicada gauze, waxed xuan paper, thin milky resin, shaved horn lamellae, frosted glass, oiled silk | `semi-translucent [material] letting the dark inner silhouette bleed through against the rim light` |
| **4. Volumetric** | light is trapped and diffused inside a fibrous or porous 3D mass | sheared beaver fur, raw cotton wadding, charred feather down, frayed wool fringe, layered horsehair braid, dry moss, spun silk floss | `volumetric [material] holding soft rim light inside its fibrous edge` |
| **+ BJD Craft Finish** | jeweller-grade sculptural relief (used once per prompt on the hero hardware) | — | `jeweller-grade fittings with sculptural surface relief and hand-finished resin-and-porcelain sheen` |

**Three-scale texture rule:** Assign at most **one macro relief** (quilted armour, sculpted metal, deep box pleats), **one mid weave** (tone-on-tone jacquard, twill, herringbone, cord ribbing), and **one micro grain** (linen slub, leather pores, hammer marks, brushed metal grain — visible in the 2×2 close-ups).

## 2. Unbundled Fabric Drape, Weight, and Hand Lexicon

Never write a fabric name without its **mechanical weight and fold behaviour**:

- **Stiff / Architectural (holds volume away from the body):** `triple-starched linen holding a rigid cylinder`, `bonded heavy wool with crisp sculptural creases`, `hemp-interlined brocade standing two centimetres clear of the collarbone`, `tar-stiffened oilskin folding in sharp angular planes`, `lacquer-infused silk holding a flared bell contour`.
- **Heavy / Dragging (pulls vertically downward):** `high-density velvet dragging the hem into a heavy floor pool`, `water-darkened coarse canvas pulling taut from the shoulder pins`, `quilted cotton padding hanging plumb from the yoke`, `oiled leather aprons weighted by brass rivets`.
- **Fluid / Bias-Cut (clings to bone points, releases into deep pleats):** `bias-cut crepe de chine catching at the collarbone and hip before dropping into vertical flutes`, `washed mulberry silk clinging to the forearm and pooling over the cuff`.
- **Crisp / Floating (lightweight, sharp edge memory):** `ultra-fine organza hovering off the skin with a curled laser-cut rim`, `stiffened gauze ribbons holding a wide S-curve in still air`.

## 5. Colour System, Mineral Pigments & Token-Frequency Quotas

### 5.1 Six structural colour relationships

Choose the relationship first, then assign **70% base / 25% anchor / 5% focal accent** + **1 structural chromatic anchor**:

| Relationship | Optical mechanism | Temperature rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monochrome + 1 Spark** | 70% and 25% share one hue family separated by a wide value gap; 5% is a high-chroma opposite | if base/anchor are cool (bone/slate), the 5% spark must be warm (cinnabar/amber), and vice versa |
| **Analogous Gradient** | three adjacent hues on the wheel stepping down in value and chroma | inject one small counter-temperature hardware metal to prevent colour wash |
| **Complementary Tension** | 70% low-chroma base vs 5–10% opposite-hue focal accent | keep the 70% base desaturated so the pair does not vibrate cheaply |
| **Split-Complementary** | one base hue + two flanking opposite hues at different values | lock one flanking hue to the 25% dark anchor and the other to the 5% focal |
| **Earth & Mineral Jewel** | raw neutral earth base (umber, ochre, ash, raw linen) + one high-purity mineral gem/pigment | use texture contrast (coarse matte earth vs tight specular mineral) |
| **Achromatic + High-Voltage Clash** | near-black/ash/bone grayscale body + one 10% industrial or toxic pigment (sulfur, verdigris, cyan) | requires at least a 2-stop value difference between the clash colour and its neighbour |

### 5.2 Cultural & mineral pigment vocabulary (replace generic `red, blue, green, black`)

- **Whites & Pales (never pure `#FFFFFF`):** `raw bone ivory (#F2EFE8)`, `chalk-white (#EFECE6)`, `oyster shell (#E8E2D6)`, `warm parchment (#E5DEC9)`, `frost-ash (#E2E6E8)`.
- **Blacks & Darks (never neutral `#000000` — always name temperature):** `blue-black lampblack (#181B20)`, `warm iron-black (#221E1D)`, `green-black pine soot (#19211E)`, `charred umber-black (#241F1A)`, `bruised plum-black (#211A22)`.
- **Reds & Rusts:** `mineral cinnabar (#8C1F2D)`, `deep oxblood (#4A151B)`, `kiln iron-rust (#7A2E22)`, `dried madder (#6E2630)`, `realgar orange-red (#B84A24)`.
- **Greens & Blues:** `slate celadon (#6E827A)`, `oxidized verdigris (#4A6B5D)`, `mineral azurite (#264366)`, `faded woad indigo (#2E3B4E)`, `deep malachite (#1E4D3B)`.
- **Earths & Metals:** `raw yellow ochre (#9E7B42)`, `burnt umber (#4E3B2C)`, `tarnished pewter (#7A7D82)`, `brushed titanium-grey (#8C9298)`, `antique brass gone brown (#735C38)`.

### 5.3 Token-frequency quota (enforced by `scripts/preflight.py`)

Models weight token frequency, not blueprint percentages. If an accent colour word appears 6 times and the base colour appears twice, the render inverts your palette.

| Role | Max occurrences of its colour word in the English prompt | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **5% focal accent** | **≤ 2** (binding in all registers) | irises/lips + at most one hero motif core |
| **25% anchor tone** | **≤ 4** | hair mass, boots, belt, or structural frame |
| **70% primary hue** | **≤ 8** (and must be **≥ 2× the accent count**) | distributed across the matte base, mid weave, and sheer/edge |
| **Any single material word** | **≤ 5** | dual colour-and-material words (`silver, gold, jade, bronze`) count **once as colour and once as material** against both caps |

### 5.4 Render-vocabulary budget

- Keep house rendering terms (`3D game CG`, `BJD porcelain`, `subsurface glow`) strictly inside **Block 1 (Header)** and **Block 3 (Skin clause)**.
- Name each rendering concept **once**. Repeating `subsurface scattering` or `intricate` in the garment body displaces character facts and triggers preflight failure.
