# Atlas: Unbundled High-Aesthetic Physical Lexicon & Colour Discipline

**Nothing in this file is bundled by genre, archetype, or character type.**

Use this lexicon during `engine/03` and `pipeline/design.md` to translate your derived design decisions into high-aesthetic, physically concrete English prose aligned with top-tier 3DCG animation and Oriental haute couture.

---

## 1. The Four Material Light Behaviours

Every costume must combine **at least two, ideally three** of the four light behaviours below so the 3D render reads as rich, tactile, and expensive — plus the **BJD finish layer** on the hero ornament:

| Light Behaviour | Optical physics | High-aesthetic unbundled materials (pick & combine freely) | Prompt phrasing mechanics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Broad soft specular (哑光柔漫射)** | light scatters across a dense, fine weave or velvet nap with a velvety, high-end matte falloff | heavy mulberry silk crepe, abyssal matte velvet with directional pile, fine cashmere-wool suiting, brushed nubuck leather, unglazed porcelain-white brocade, matte cold-dyed linen | `rich matte [material] with a velvety hand and soft grazing light falloff` / `deep [material] absorbing the key light across its nap` |
| **2. Tight specular (锐利高光切面)** | a razor-sharp, jewel-like highlight along a polished bevel, metallic wire, or gemstone surface | blackened gold (乌金) filigree, cold-forged dark silver, carved mutton-fat white jade, translucent ink-jade (墨玉), blood-agate, wet volcanic obsidian, mirror-polished lacquer, gilded bronze | `catching a razor-thin specular highlight along the sculpted bevel` / `polished [material] throwing a crisp pinpoint reflection` |
| **3. Translucent (流光半透/雾透)** | light passes through and scatters inside sheer layers, creating luminous depth and haloing at the edges | iridescent siren-gauze (鲛绡), moonlit cicada-wing organza (蝉翼纱), smoke-gradient silk chiffon, frost-veined sheer tulle, thin translucent horn or frost-jade lamellae | `semi-translucent [material] letting the inner silhouette bleed through against the rim light` / `layered sheer [material] with a luminous edge falloff` |
| **4. Volumetric (蓬松空气感/绒感)** | light is trapped and diffused inside fine plumes, fur, or spun filaments | snow-fox fur trim, charred raven-feather plumes, spun silver-silk tassels, layered frost-chiffon ruffles, fine gossamer fringe | `volumetric [material] holding soft rim light inside its fine feathery edge` |
| **+ BJD Craft Finish** | jeweller-grade sculptural relief (used once per prompt on the hero hardware) | — | `jeweller-grade fittings with sculptural surface relief and hand-finished resin-and-porcelain sheen` |

**Three-scale texture rule:** Assign **one macro relief** (sculpted metal armour/filigree, deep pleated structure), **one mid weave** (tone-on-tone jacquard damask, metallic-thread brocade, pleated fluting), and **one micro grain** (fine silk weave, jade clouding, brushed metal grain — visible in the 2×2 close-ups).

## 2. Unbundled Fabric Drape, Weight, and Motion Lexicon

Always pair a fabric name with how it **sculpts the body or moves in space**:

- **Sculpted / Tailored (carves the waist, shoulder, and collar):** `structured heavy brocade holding a sharp standing collar`, `boned matte-velvet waist girdle sculpting a narrow hourglass cinch`, `layered shoulder cloud-yoke (yunjian) holding a crisp architectural contour`, `tailored dark-silk surcoat with razor-sharp lapel edges`.
- **Fluid / Bias-Cut (clings to curves, flows into long vertical lines):** `fluid bias-cut duchesse satin skimming the hip and dropping into floor-length vertical flutes`, `heavy crepe silk hanging plumb from the high waist-cinch to elongate the leg line`, `high-slit overskirt parting cleanly to reveal the inner leg and fitted boot`.
- **Ethereal / Cascading (sheer outer layers that create depth without bulk):** `ultra-sheer smoke-organza over-robe floating two centimetres off the dark inner gown`, `cascading gradient-dyed gauze sleeves trailing past the fingertips with weightless translucency`, `split back-panel streamers catching the rim light behind the shoulders`.

## 4. Unbundled Narrative & Transcendent Trace Lexicon

In high-aesthetic fantasy and drama, character traces are **story-driven, poetic, or supernatural**, never ugly dirt or mundane grime:

- **Cultivation / Power Traces (功法与超凡异象):** `faint frost-crystal rime along the lower sleeve edge`, `hair tips gradually turning to translucent snow-silver`, `a hairline golden curse-sigil glowing faintly beneath the left collarbone`, `singed sheer hem edges dissolving into dark-gold ember flecks`, `fingertips tinted with a subtle obsidian-jade sheen`.
- **Combat & Fate Marks (宿命与战斗印记):** `a clean blade-slashed hem repaired with fine gold-thread embroidery`, `one half-shattered jade hairpin held together by a silver wire wrap`, `a faint, clean silver scar line crossing the left brow or jaw angle`, `a blood-red silk binding cord wrapped tightly around one wrist`.
- **High-Aesthetic Skin & Eye Details:** `velvet-matte porcelain cheek planes contrasting with a sharp specular line along the nose bridge`, `a faint crimson or gold-dust smudge at the outer eye corner`, `cool translucent skin showing a hint of pale-jade spiritual veins at the temple`.

## 5. Colour System, Mineral Pigments & Token-Frequency Quotas

### 5.1 Six high-aesthetic colour relationships

Choose the relationship first, then assign **70% base / 25% anchor / 5% focal accent** + **1 structural chromatic anchor**:

| Relationship | Optical mechanism | Temperature rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monochrome + 1 High-Chroma Spark** | 70% and 25% share one hue family separated by a wide value gap; 5% is a piercing opposite spark | if base/anchor are cool (moon-white / abyssal blue-black), the 5% spark is warm (cinnabar / molten gold), and vice versa |
| **Dark Abyssal + Luminous Jade/Gold** | 70% deep temperature-biased black/obsidian + 25% cold silver/bone-white + 5% crimson or molten gold | classic high-status demonic or night-sovereign hierarchy; separate dark layers by matte velvet vs sheer gauze vs tight metal specular |
| **Ethereal Light + Deep Ink Anchor** | 70% layered moon-white/celadon/frost-grey + 25% blue-black hair/boots/corset + 5% jewel accent | classic immortal or noble hierarchy; never put white on white without a dark structural waist or collar anchor |
| **Complementary Tension** | 70% low-chroma deep hue (e.g. muted celadon or bruised plum) vs 5–10% opposite-hue focal gem | keep the 70% base sophisticated and low-saturation so the focal gem commands attention |
| **Split-Complementary Luxe** | one primary base hue + two flanking contrasting tones at different values | lock one to the 25% structural anchor and the other to the 5% iris/chest focal |
| **Achromatic + Phantom Accent** | bone-white, silver-grey, and ink-black body + one 5–10% supernatural hue (phantom violet, ice-cyan, blood-agate) | requires at least a 2-stop value difference between the accent and its surrounding cloth |

### 5.2 Oriental fantasy pigment vocabulary (replace generic `red, blue, green, black, white`)

- **Luminous Whites & Pales (`#EAE6DF` to `#FAF8F4`):** `moon-white (月白 #F2F4F6)`, `warm bone-ivory (骨白 #F2EFE8)`, `mutton-fat jade white (羊脂白 #F4F1EA)`, `frost-silver (霜银 #E2E6EA)`, `pearl-mist (珠光雾白 #EDEAE4)`.
- **Abyssal Blacks & Darks (always name temperature, never flat `#000000`):** `abyssal blue-black (鸦青/玄黑 #161820)`, `warm obsidian-black (乌金黑 #1E1B1A)`, `pine-soot green-black (松烟墨 #18201E)`, `bruised night-plum (幽冥紫黑 #211824)`.
- **Cinnabars, Crimsons & Plums:** `imperial cinnabar (朱砂 #9E1F28)`, `deep blood-agate (血珀红 #6E1420)`, `dark wine-crimson (绛红 #541622)`, `phantom wisteria-violet (幻紫 #6C5B7B)`.
- **Celadons, Jades & Azurites:** `misty sky-celadon (天青 #7A948E)`, `deep寒玉冰青 (frost-jade #5C7A78)`, `mineral azurite blue (石青 #243E5C)`, `abyssal teal (碧渊 #1B3B3A)`.

### 5.3 Token-frequency quota (enforced by `scripts/preflight.py`)

| Role | Max occurrences of its colour word in the English prompt | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **5% focal accent** | **≤ 2** (binding in all registers) | irises/lips + at most one hero motif core |
| **25% anchor tone** | **≤ 4** | hair mass, boots, waist corset, or structural hardware |
| **70% primary hue** | **≤ 8** (and must be **≥ 2× the accent count**) | distributed across matte base, mid weave, and sheer layer |
| **Any single material word** | **≤ 5** | dual colour-and-material words (`silver, gold, jade, bronze`) count **once as colour and once as material** |
