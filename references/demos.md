# Design Demos

Three calibrated examples of the full design-mode delivery: two editorial, one glamour. They are calibration, not templates; replace every subject-specific detail.

**Both demos carry all seven blueprint points, and point 5 is counted rather than described.** That is the standard: a blueprint with six points is an unfinished design, regardless of how good the prompt reads. The seven points and their sources are specified in [pipeline/design.md](pipeline/design.md) §3.

The prompts run long because a seven-panel sheet needs the budget described in [pipeline/design.md](pipeline/design.md) §4.

## Why there are three, and why they must not overlap

Two examples calibrate a line; three calibrate a space. Two demos resolving in the same genre, subgenre, gear, and silhouette share five of nine batch axes, and they become the strongest signal in the prompt context — the calibration then teaches one design instead of a method.

**The three demos below clear the batch check against each other** — axes 1 (silhouette) and 2 (key colour family) differ pairwise, as `contract/gates.md` §9 requires. Use them as evidence that the axes can move, never as a menu of three looks to pick from. A fourth character that resembles any of them on the mandatory axes is a failure of Layer 0, not a reuse.

| Demo | Register | Silhouette | Key colour | Topology | Light | Trim | Identity anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | editorial | hourglass | bone-white + cinnabar | bone-sculpted | high-key pearlescent | narrow tarnished-silver filigree | facial index 1.52, narrow mouth, equal lips |
| 2 | glamour | bell / A-line | pearl-white + crimson | top-peak cascade | high-key pearlescent | silver filigree band | broad short face, wide mouth, very full lower lip |
| 3 | editorial | cocoon | earth-umber + verdigris | languid negative space | warm tactile GI | tarred rope and brass | facial index 1.30, broad jaw, wide-set eyes |

**Pairwise clearance.** Silhouette and key colour differ in all three pairs, which is the hard requirement. Demos 1 and 2 still share the light mode and the trim material — both are legal, because demo 2 is glamour and takes the register's native light, and the pair clears the rest: silhouette, key colour, topology, register, motif, and identity anchor. Demo 3 clears six of nine against each of the others, and exists specifically to prove the space is not two points wide.

Demo 2's bell read is set by its own bodice-and-full-skirt construction, not chosen to avoid a collision. When a pair fails the batch check, record the **pair** — a character that fails alone is a design problem; a pair that fails is a system problem.

## Demo 1 — Editorial (The Deposed Lotus Goddess)

### Blueprint

1. **锁定**: Xianxia / Clan-Born / mineral bone-white with one cinnabar accent and a blue-black anchor / hourglass / **gear 2 Signature** / **editorial**. Aesthetic module: Xianxia — Clan-Born. Levers: material transgression, deconstruction.

2. **角色事实**: a lotus-cult goddess who lost her temple and kept her manners; cares about ritual correctness, compromises on her hem; state is the morning after exile, still damp. Two imperfections: water-stained hem, one chipped petal. Contradiction direction: **1 Sacred / Desecrated**. Thesis: *a deposed goddess wearing the temple she outlived, thorns grown through water-stained lotus silk.*

3. **母题种子**: `thorns growing through lotus silk`. Support: the collar and train carry it, the filigree re-fastens the chest cutout. Pressure: lands at the chest cutout and the hem, which is where both imperfections sit. Hands: empty and open, one raised to the still-open collar.

4. **重心**: Topology 5 Bone-Sculpted Tension; density at chest cutout and train; back resolves as one chain from crown to split hem. Hero: chest thorn filigree. Supporting: crown, back chain, train petals.

5. **装饰密度（计数）**:
   - **层数 4**, in order: a close blue-black inner gown showing a 2cm line at the collar and wrists (skin-adjacent, darkest value) / a fitted bone-white jacquard body (structural) / a water-stained raw-silk skirt carrying the palette (main) / petal-layered translucent organza sleeves and train (outer). Each differs from its neighbour in value or opacity: dark under light, matte under translucent.
   - **描边率 8/11 ≈ 73%**, one trim language: `collar, cuffs and both hem edges banded in the same narrow tarnished-silver thorn filigree`. Treated: collar, both cuffs, both skirt hems, chest cutout (re-fastened), both slit edges, train edge. Deliberately raw: the inner gown's wrist line, the boot top, the underside of the organza. Raw edges are the humble ones.
   - **节点 4** = 1 hero + 3 supporting, descending: chest thorn filigree around one cinnabar gem (hero, structural job: closes the cutout) > crown of tarnished lotus buds (marks rank) > back chain, crown to split train (joins two pieces) > three sculpted lotus petals at the train, one chipped (terminates the train).
   - **花型 banded → fielded**: the lotus-petal motif at three scales — macro as the chest filigree and the train petals, mid as a band at the collar and both hems, micro as a tone-on-tone jacquard repeat across the whole body field.

6. **审美秩序**: hourglass; values `#F2EFE8` bone-white light mass / `#1A1B1F` blue-black hair mass / `#BFAFC4` midtone bridge / cinnabar `#8C1F2D` focal; palette = monochrome bone-white + one cinnabar accent; chromatic anchor = the cinnabar gem in a dark silver setting; materials = matte raw ivory silk with visible slub, raised bone-on-bone jacquard, translucent organza, tarnished silver (broad soft / tight specular / translucent); focal point at the throat; open area = bare shoulders and collarbone; **rule broken = the organza petals behave like carved stone rather than wet cloth**.

7. **物理渲染与微妆**: 8.5-head, narrow shoulders, long waist, narrow hips; low sculpted cheekbones; smooth porcelain skin with a soft subsurface glow, pore hint reserved for the macro cell; large almond eyes with a defined upper lid line and a clear crease; ruby-glass irises `#8C1230` with radial fibre, a limbal ring, a secondary reflection and a wet lower lid; minimal nose bridge; soft gradient berry lip `#7E2B3A` with a visible cupid's bow and one dry flake; faint cinnabar under the lower lids; sculpted strand clumps with a soft anime highlight band and wisps across the left brow; bottom-right macro = lower lids tighten, one mouth corner lifts three millimetres and settles. Light: Mode 1 high-key pearlescent, routed through sheet-safe exposure. Engine: Midjourney, target canvas 2560×1440 (composition target; MJ native tops at 2048), `--ar 16:9`.

**Judged** (an eye, not a count): silhouette reads at 10% — an hourglass with a chest gap and a three-petal train break. Grayscale gives a bone-white light mass against blue-black hair with one cinnabar focal at the throat. Face wins; the crown and filigree sit quieter than the eyes. The one risk reads as a risk: stone-carved organza. One open area: the shoulders.

## Demo 2 — Glamour (The Snow-Crowned Immortal)

### Blueprint

1. **锁定**: Xianxia / Clan-Born / pearl-white and silver with one crimson accent / bell / **gear 2 Signature** / **glamour**. Aesthetic module: Xianxia — Clan-Born read through the glamour register. Levers: volume extreme, accumulation, material transgression.

2. **角色事实**: a snow-crowned immortal who descends for a wedding — she commands lineage rather than income; what she refuses is the floor; what she is capable of is a decision the face does not show. **Imperfections: zero by design.** Contradiction direction: **7 Sacred / Dangerous** — flawlessness plus crimson irises that read as a warning. Thesis: *a snow-crowned immortal who came down the mountain for a wedding: a hundred layers of pearl gauze and silver filigree.*

3. **母题种子**: `Snow-Crowned` (material family — correct for glamour, because the script supplies the biography). Support: the crown and the hair carry it. Pressure: the crown of the head and the collarbone. Hands: one lifted to the hair, one loose and empty.

4. **重心**: Topology 1 **Top-Peak Cascade** — crown, hair and chest carry everything, chiffon thins to the floor. **Hair is the hero**, with two peer head groups (crown + ear and hair tassels).

5. **装饰密度（计数）**:
   - **层数 5**: a dove-grey inner slip showing a band at the collar and wrist (skin-adjacent) / a fitted pearl-white high-collared bodice (structural) / a sheer petal overskirt ending above the skirt hem (main) / a translucent pearl gauze stole cut shorter than the skirt beneath (outer) / two floating silver sashes to the ankle (floating). Neighbours differ in opacity and value throughout.
   - **描边率 9/11 ≈ 82%**, one trim language: `collar, cuffs, placket and every hem banded in the same narrow silver filigree`.
   - **节点 8, descending**: silver starflower crown (hero, marks rank) > crystal drop tassels to the collarbone > starflower earring pair, counted as two (drops) > a wing-shaped chest node around one crimson gem (closes the placket) > a smaller starflower clasp at the belt (closes the belt) > a silver star chain across the shoulder blades (joins the two back panels) > one pin at the left temple. No nodes at or below the hem.
   - **花型 fielded**: the five-petal motif at three scales — macro as the crown centre and the chest node, mid as the filigree band at every edge, micro as a tone-on-tone silver-thread repeat across the whole skirt field.

6. **审美秩序**: bell / A-line — a fitted bodice over a skirt that widens from the hip, deliberately not the hourglass demo 1 uses; values `#FAF8F4` pearl-white light mass / `#17161A` blue-black hair mass / `#C9CBD2` silver midtone / crimson `#8E1B33` focal; palette = monochrome pearl-white + one crimson accent; chromatic anchor = the crimson gem in its silver mount; materials = pearl gauze, ruffled chiffon, silver filigree, crystal, silver lace boots (translucent / tight specular / broad soft); focal point at the face; density descends head → hem; **rule broken: the ornament node count — eight nodes instead of four (the earring pair counts as two)**, which is legal in glamour and fatal in editorial.

7. **物理渲染与微妆**: 8.5-head, sloped shoulders, narrow waist, long legs, slim hips; small heart-shaped face, pointed chin; very large crimson glass irises `#8E1B33` with radial fibre, a dark limbal ring, a bright secondary reflection and a wet lower lid, a defined upper lid line, winged liner, wine lips `#6E1E30` with a glossy centre and a visible cupid's bow, one small mole, one blush, flawless luminous porcelain skin; centre part with a high crown, side-swept curtains, silver-white inner streaks written as sculpted strand clumps with a soft anime highlight band, silver starflower crown with crystal drop tassels to the collarbone; bottom-right macro = small closed-mouth smile, eyes warm and narrowed, one brow softening. Light: Mode 1 high-key pearlescent through sheet-safe exposure. Engine: Midjourney, target canvas 3072×1728 (composition target; MJ native tops at 2048), `--ar 16:9`.

### Preflight result

**Counted** (via `scripts/preflight.py`):

Zero imperfections with the direction-7 signal carried by crimson irises. Charm present: one askew temple pin, a turn to three-quarter in the top-right cell, two catchlights, a glossed lip, one matte cheek. **Why it breaks editorial rules on purpose**: zero imperfections, the Sacred/Dangerous contradiction rather than a lived one, an ornament in every zone, hair as the hero rather than one worn ornament, and the default moon-white palette. All five are correct here and fatal in editorial. The flawless surface and crimson irises read as a warning, not as editorial Sacred/Desecrated. Note the density gradient: crown and hair pin at the top, filigree and gem at the chest, then plain chiffon to the floor. "Flawless" is material flawless: porcelain clarity, a controlled specular, iris fibre with two reflections, and sculpted strand clumps, because a flat untextured face is not perfection, it is a sticker.

## Demo 3 — Editorial (The Tally Clerk Who Counted Correctly)

This demo exists to prove the design space is not two points wide. It differs from demos 1 and 2 on every batch axis it can, and its seed is **derived from the script's conflict**, not taken from the §1B library — see [pipeline/design.md](pipeline/design.md) §2, layer 2.

### Blueprint

1. **锁定**: Republican / Modern Historical — working class / earth umber with one verdigris accent and a tarred black anchor / cocoon / **gear 2 Signature** / **editorial**. Aesthetic module: Republican / Modern Historical. Levers: deconstruction, material transgression.

2. **角色事实**: a wharf tally clerk, 1938, who is still counting cargo accurately while the company ships its own records out ahead of the city. He cares about exactness and has given up on his cuff. State: a long shift in rain, coat hem water-dark, one knuckle scraped on a crate lid. Two imperfections: the salt-bloomed hem, the turned raw cuff. Contradiction direction: **2 Cheap / Exact** — a pressed, accurate collar above a coat that has stopped being maintained. Thesis: *a tally clerk who counted the cargo correctly while it was shipped away: a tarred shoulder cape worn one size too large, its strap gone.*

3. **母题种子**: derived, not library-sourced. **`The Strap With Nothing Left to Hold`** — conflict: he is still signing for a cargo that is leaving; cost in daily action: he checks, counts, and sets things down; compulsory class: the coat is issued and cannot be replaced. Support: the oversized tarred oilskin shoulder cape, its tally-board strap worn smooth at the left shoulder with no board on it. Pressure: the left shoulder and the water-dark hem. Hands: empty, one thumb counting against the same hand's fingers, the other flat at the side.

4. **重心**: Topology 4 **Languid & Uncinched Negative Space** — the volume sits low and forward, the cape pools rather than falls, nothing is cinched. Back resolves as a loose billowing back with the strap-wear mark at the left shoulder and one rope-knot band across the yoke.

5. **装饰密度（计数）**:
   - **层数 3** (editorial working band 2–3): a damp grey cotton undershirt showing a band at the collar and both cuffs (skin-adjacent) / a coarse matte umber canvas work coat carrying the palette (main) / an oversized tarred oilskin shoulder cape cut shorter than the coat beneath so the inner hem reads (outer).    - **花型 banded** (working-character band): the rope-knot motif at three scales — macro as the tarred piping itself, mid as one heavy-thread band across the back yoke, micro as a tone-on-tone stamped repeat on the inner shirt collar. Banded, not fielded, and that is the choice.
   - **Batch note**: editorial's node count is a cap of 4, so it cannot be used to differ from demo 1. The differentiation is carried by layers (3 vs 4), edge rate (62% vs 73%), and coverage (banded vs fielded).

6. **审美秩序**: cocoon; values `#E8E2D6` salt-bloomed canvas light mass / `#241F1A` tarred cape anchor / `#8A6F52` umber midtone / verdigris `#4A5F52` focal; palette = earth and jewel (earth umber ground + one verdigris accent); chromatic anchor = the verdigris-stained brass hook at the throat; materials = coarse matte canvas, tarred oilskin, oxidized brass (broad soft / tight specular / none translucent); focal point at the throat and the worn strap; open area = bare forearms and the open collar; **rule broken = the oilskin cape holds an armour-like stiff fold instead of draping**.

7. **物理渲染与微妆**: 7.5-to-8-head athletic frame, slack square shoulders, thick waist, heavy hips; broad short face with weight in the lower half; facial index 1.30, mouth width 0.86, inter-canthal 1.12, eye width to face 1.05, iris fill 0.80, nose length 0.34 with a broad tip, philtrum 0.46, lip ratio 1:1.5 pressed and chapped, chin width 1.00, cheekbone low and soft; large almond eyes with a defined upper lid line and a clear crease; dull warm-brown irises with radial fibre, a darker limbal ring, a secondary reflection and a wet meniscus; sun-weathered skin with a soft subsurface glow and a real sweat sheen, pore hint reserved for the macro cell; thinning ash-grey hair two centimetres too long with sculpted strand clumps and one escaping temple strand; bottom-right macro = one nostril flaring slightly, the mouth staying shut, the eyes not moving.
