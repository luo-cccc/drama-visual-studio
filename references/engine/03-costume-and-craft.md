# Engine 03: Costume Derivation, IFV, and the Five Haute-Couture Operators

Layers 2–5 of the derivation stack. This file turns the character's **Dao, power signature, and dramatic conflict** into a **visual motif seed**, locks the **IFV triple**, and applies **five inline haute-couture & aesthetic operators** (Proportion, Rhythm, Notan, Colour, Structural Grounding).

Coordinate systems live in [../atlas/coordinates.md](../atlas/coordinates.md), density bands in [../atlas/density.md](../atlas/density.md), high-aesthetic unbundled terms in [../atlas/lexicon.md](../atlas/lexicon.md), and subgenre aesthetic anchors in [../atlas/genre-traps.md](../atlas/genre-traps.md).

## 1. Conflict & Dao-to-Seed Compiler & the IFV Triple

### 1.1 Derive the seed from the character's Dao and conflict (5 steps)

In Oriental fantasy and high-aesthetic drama, a motif seed is **one striking visual metaphor that fuses the character's power/Dao with their dramatic tension** — never a mundane peasant tool, and never a generic page-game outfit.

1. **Name their Dao/Power + Dramatic Conflict.** Not just "a demonic protector", but "an abyssal enforcer who binds enemies' souls with resonant blackened-gold bone chimes while maintaining untouchable aristocratic composure."
2. **Name the visual contrast between their outer poise and their lethal power.** How does their power manifest on silk, jade, metal, and skin?
3. **Name the signature structural garment piece that channels or restrains this power.** (e.g. a spinal metal armature, a throat-locking jade gorget, cascading sheer sleeves hiding articulated claw-guards, a high-waisted corseted seal-girdle).
4. **Compress steps 1–3 into a 4-to-7-word high-aesthetic visual seed** — e.g. `Blackened-Gold Chimes Threading Abyssal Silk`, `Frost-Jade Fractured by Immortal Fire`, `Spinal Bone-Armature Under Moonlit Gauze`.
5. **Run the three growth rules (§1.2).**

### 1.2 The three growth rules (mandatory for every seed)

Answer all three in blueprint point 3 before writing any garment clause:

| Rule | Question | High-Aesthetic Output in the Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Support (高定承托结构)** | Which sculpted garment structure or luxury hardware piece physically anchors the seed on the body? | high-waisted openwork metal corset-girdle, sculpted cloud-shoulder yoke (`yunjian`), articulated spinal clasp-chain, high standing throat-collar, or single-shoulder armored pauldron |
| **2. Pressure / Release (张力与倾泻点)** | Where is the body most strictly bound, and where does the fabric or power release into dramatic flow or a high slit? | e.g., throat and waist strictly locked in blackened gold while the left thigh slit and sheer over-sleeves part cleanly to reveal ink-jade skin or inner boots |
| **3. Empty hands (空手气场姿态)** | How do the empty hands express command, poise, or latent lethality without holding a prop? | connects directly to the pose vector ([01-truth-and-pose.md](01-truth-and-pose.md) §7); e.g., articulated claw-rings on one hand resting lightly on the waist jade, or two fingers touching a hanging temple chain |

**Two derivation traces (showing high-aesthetic Oriental fantasy mechanics):**

- *Trace 1 (Xianxia — Demonic-Path Enforcer)*: Dao & Conflict = commands the Abyssal Phantom-Chime punishment, executing apostates with silent grace → Seed = **`Blackened-Gold Spine Chimes Over Abyssal Velvet`** → Support = a high-waisted openwork blackened-gold corset-girdle and high collar-gorget over a deep night-plum and obsidian-black velvet high-slit long coat → Pressure/Release = waist and throat locked tight, while translucent smoke-gauze over-sleeves and a high left-leg slit open to show fitted dark boots and ink-jade tinted fingertips → Hands = left hand wearing articulated blackened-gold finger-guards resting lightly at the hip jade, right hand raised near the collarbone.
- *Trace 2 (Xianxia — Tribulation-Marked Sword Immortal)*: Dao & Conflict = survived heavenly frost-lightning by sealing the bolt inside her own sword-bone → Seed = **`Astral Gold Sealing Fractured Frost-Jade`** → Support = an asymmetrical carved mutton-fat jade shoulder-and-rib armature mended with fine molten-gold seams over layered moon-white and sky-celadon siren-gauze → Pressure/Release = right shoulder locked in gold-seamed jade while the left sleeve cascades in weightless frost-rimmed organza → Hands = one hand tucked behind the back, the other lightly brushing the jade waist-pendant.

### 1.3 Lock the IFV triple (Invariant / Free / Forbidden)

Check [../atlas/genre-traps.md](../atlas/genre-traps.md) for the locked subgenre and declare:

- **Invariant (1 item):** The one high-fashion structural anchor from Support/Pressure that defines this character.
- **Free (2–3 items):** Which garment zones are open for creative exploration in §2 (e.g. `inner collar cutout shape, back train split geometry, hair ornament drop depth`).
- **Forbidden (3 items):** Three low-aesthetic traps banned from this character (e.g. `no mundane peasant/butcher leather aprons or hemp rags, no bulky page-game shoulder spikes, no copied under-eye tear-mole`).

## 2. The Five Inline Craft Operators

Counts alone make a design compliant; these five operators make it look like **top-tier commercial animation and haute-couture key art**.

### 2.1 Operator: Proportion & Flattering Cut (`craft: proportion`)

**Question:** *How does the cut maximize visual charisma, leg length, and silhouette authority?*

Never cut a character into boxy 1:1 halves or hide their figure under shapeless sacks:

1. **Elongated Head-Count Band:**
   - Default to **`8.0-to-8.5-head` slender idealized fashion proportion** for adult仙侠/玄幻/高定 characters so the figure reads as tall, graceful, and commanding. Use `7.5-to-8-head` only for compact martial youths or grounded modern roles.
2. **High Waist-Cinch & Leg Elongation (Golden / Root Split ~ 3 : 5 upper-to-lower):**
   - Place the visual waist-cinch (corseted girdle, metal waist-seal, or high-tied sash) **slightly above the anatomical waist** to create an elongated **3:5 upper-to-lower body ratio**.
   - Even in a loose `Cocoon` or `Languid` silhouette, expose **one clear structural anchor** (collarbone V-neck, slender wrists, or a high side-slit showing the leg/boot line) so the body beneath stays desirable and readable.
3. **Staggered Hem & Slit Geometry:**
   - Combine **long vertical trailing lines** (floor-sweeping back panels, long sashes, cascading outer sleeves) with **sharp negative-space cutouts** (a high thigh slit on one side, an asymmetric mid-calf front cut against a floor-length back train, or fitted forearm bracers emerging from slit wide sleeves).

### 2.2 Operator: Rhythm, Hierarchy & The Breakpoint (`craft: rhythm`)

**Question:** *Why does one ornate design read as effortless luxury, while another looks like noisy clutter?*

1. **Rule of Three & Scale Gap:** Use **1 dominant hero ornament + 2 or 3 clearly subordinate supports** at most one-third the hero's size. Never line up four equal-sized brooches or four equal-width stripes.
2. **Unequal Layer Stepping:** Offset adjacent garment layers at **unequal intervals** (e.g. a 2cm contrasting inner collar rim, an 8cm sculpted cloud-yoke, a 30cm sheer outer sleeve drop).
3. **The Mandatory Breakpoint & Breathing Zone (破律与留白):**
   - Every symmetrical silhouette must have **one deliberate asymmetrical break** (a single-side shoulder armour piece, an off-centre high slit, a single long temple tassel on the left side).
   - Every richly patterned costume must preserve **one primary open breathing area (留白呼吸区)** — e.g. a clean V-neck of porcelain collarbones, or an unpatterned expanse of deep matte velvet across the upper torso — so the eye rests and the face wins.

### 2.3 Operator: Notan — 10% Value Masses (`craft: notan`)

**Question:** *At 10% thumbnail size in grayscale, does the figure read immediately as an iconic silhouette?*

Assign the figure into **three unequal grayscale value blocks**:

1. **70 / 25 / 5 Value Split:**
   - **70% Dominant Value Mass:** either Deep Abyssal Dark (`#161820` to `#211824` — obsidian, night-plum, blue-black) or Luminous Light (`#EAE6DF` to `#F4F1EA` — moon-white, bone-ivory, frost-silver) or Rich Mid-Tone (`#5C7A78` frost-jade / `#6E1420` blood-agate).
   - **25% Counter-Value Structural Anchor:** carves the waist, shoulder, and hair outline against the 70% mass (e.g. a pale mutton-fat jade and silver shoulder-yoke + porcelain skin cutting against an abyssal-black coat; or a blue-black corseted waist-girdle and dark hair framing a moon-white gown).
   - **5% High-Contrast Spark:** at the eyes and throat.
2. **Focal Contrast Lock at Face & Throat:** The sharpest light-against-dark edge on the entire sheet **must frame the face and throat**. If a dark-haired character wears an abyssal-black robe, open a V-neck of luminous porcelain skin or insert a pale jade/silver inner collar rim so the head never merges into the dark torso.

### 2.4 Operator: Colour Architecture & Temperature Clash (`craft: colour`)

**Question:** *How do we make classic Eastern fantasy palettes (玄黑、月白、天青、绛红、鎏金) look fresh, expensive, and distinct across a cast?*

Never ban the genre's beloved palettes — elevate them through **triple-texture splitting and temperature clash**:

1. **Chroma Hierarchy:** Keep the 70% fabric mass at refined mineral/silk saturation so the **5% jewel iris/lip/focal accent** and **structural chromatic anchor** command the viewer's eye.
2. **Mandatory Counter-Temperature Spark (冷暖对撞):**
   - In a cool palette (abyssal blue-black, frost-silver, celadon, phantom violet), inject **one warm high-craft spark** (molten dark-gold filigree `#C99A2E`, imperial cinnabar `#9E1F28`, or warm blood-agate).
   - In a warm palette (crimson, warm bone-ivory, blackened gold), inject **one cold mineral interrupt** (translucent ink-jade, cold tarnished silver, or frost-cyan gem).
3. **Triple-Texture Split within the 70% Hue:**
   - Split the primary colour across **three distinct light behaviours** ([../atlas/lexicon.md](../atlas/lexicon.md) §1):
     - **Matte velvet or heavy silk crepe** (absorbs light, grounds the core)
     - **Tone-on-tone jacquard damask or metallic brocade** (catches grazing light at the folds)
     - **Iridescent sheer siren-gauze or cicada-organza** (haloes the outer silhouette with rim light)
4. **Token-Frequency Discipline:** Enforce [../atlas/lexicon.md](../atlas/lexicon.md) §5.3 caps (`accent ≤ 2, anchor ≤ 4, base ≤ 8, single material ≤ 5`).

### 2.5 Operator: Haute-Couture Structural Grounding (`craft: grounding`)

**Question:** *Why does this look like a Million-Dollar 3DCG Animation asset rather than vague AI cloth soup?*

In Oriental fantasy, "grounding" means **haute-couture tailoring precision and jeweller-grade hardware mechanics** — never peasant burlap or clumsy bamboo cages:

1. **Sculpted Fit & Armature Logic:**
   - State how structured pieces hold their form: `a high-waisted corset-girdle boned with blackened gold`, `an openwork silver cloud-shoulder armature pinned to the collarbones`, `a standing court collar stiffened with silk-wrapped stays`.
   - State how heavy ornaments attach to sheer or fluid cloth: `suspended from a braided dark-gold shoulder chain`, `anchored to the structured brocade under-bodice`, `clasping the split cape across the sternum`.
2. **Specific Oriental Fantasy Tailoring:**
   - Replace generic `robe` or `dress` with concrete couture cuts from [../atlas/lexicon.md](../atlas/lexicon.md) §3: `three-layered overlapping cross-collar (jiaoling) with a narrow V-neck`, `high-waisted knife-pleated long skirt with a left-thigh slit`, `sleeveless tailored surcoat over wide slit gauze sleeves and fitted metal vambraces`.
3. **Graceful Drape Physics:**
   - Describe how gravity and motion act on the fabrics: `heavy matte velvet pulling plumb from the waist-cinch while sheer smoke-organza over-sleeves float two centimetres off the arms`.

## 3. Boldness Levers, Glamour Hair, and I2V Adaptation

### 3.1 The ten high-aesthetic boldness levers

Select **2–3 levers at gear 2 Signature**, **3–4 at gear 3 Avant-garde**, **up to 5 at gear 4 Surreal** ([../contract/gates.md](../contract/gates.md) §3):

1. **Proportion elongation**: extreme high-waisted leg extension, towering sculpted collar framing the jaw, floor-sweeping sleeves or train.
2. **Sheer-vs-Armour inversion**: feather-light translucent siren-gauze paired directly with cold blackened-steel or jade spinal/shoulder armour.
3. **High-voltage colour clash**: a 90% abyssal or bone-white monochrome pierced by a single 10% supernatural hue (phantom violet, blood-agate, ice-cyan, molten gold).
4. **Luxury material transgression**: garments woven from non-cloth fantasy media — translucent frost-jade lamellae, spun liquid silver, charred raven-plumes, crystallized ember-silk.
5. **Silhouette hybrid**: fusing classical immortal drapery with sharp modern haute-couture corsetry, cape-harnesses, or asymmetrical tailcoat cuts.
6. **Scale shift**: one monumental architectural crown, spinal frame, or shoulder piece contrasted against micro-fine filigree chains.
7. **Surgical / Aesthetic deconstruction**: deliberate collarbone, spinal, or side-waist cutouts spanned by fine silver chains or jade bridges.
8. **Rhythmic accumulation**: a cascading array of fine silver chime-tassels or linked jade vertebrae descending from crown to waist.
9. **Transcendent body trace**: fingertips transitioning into translucent ink-jade, hair tips turning to snow-frost, or a glowing golden seal under the skin.
10. **Levitating upper-zone element**: a fractured jade halo, floating metallic talisman blades, or weightless crown ribbons hovering 2cm above the shoulders/head.

### 3.2 Glamour hair system & selling-point order

Specify all **six layers** when writing a `glamour` character (and use at least four in `editorial` fantasy):

| Layer | What to decide | High-Aesthetic Tokens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Mass & length** | total volume and drop depth | `ankle-grazing ink-black tresses`, `waist-length cascading silver-white hair`, `high-bound martial tail trailing to the hips` |
| **2. Crown architecture** | top silhouette height | `high-puffed noble crown`, `sleek centre-parted court symmetry`, `swept up into a sculpted jade-pinned updo` |
| **3. Face framing** | how locks sculpt the cheek and jaw | `long side-swept temple locks narrowing the jawline`, `one silk-fine lock curving across the cheek`, `tucked behind one ear to expose an ear-chain` |
| **4. Strand physics** | mandatory house craft | `grouped locks with sculpted strand clumps, a soft anime-style highlight band across the crown, individual flyaway strands catching the backlight` |
| **5. Value interruption** | linear contrast inside the hair | `frost-silver inner underlayer streaks`, `fine braided dark-gold hair chains`, `crimson-tinted hair tips` |
| **6. Headpiece inventory** | 2–4 distinct jeweller-grade pieces | e.g., `openwork blackened-gold hairpin with agate drop chains to the jawline` |

**Selling-point priority:** `1. Face (§1A+§1B) → 2. Hair (6 layers) → 3. Elongated Silhouette & Waist/Slit Cut → 4. Hero Throat/Chest Node → 5. Matte-vs-Sheer Layering → 6. Back-View Spine Resolution → 7. Micro Damask Weave.`

### 3.3 I2V (Image-to-Video) adaptation rules

For single-frame video generation assets: shorten floor-sweeping trains to calf-length split hems, bind loose waist-length hair into a high ponytail or structured coil, bind wide sleeves at the forearm, and shorten hanging tassels above the collarbone.
