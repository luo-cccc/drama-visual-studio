# Engine 01: Character Essence, High-Aesthetic Truth, and Orthographic Pose

Layer 1 of the derivation stack. Use this file to extract or construct the character's core Dao/power, charisma archetype, dramatic contradiction, design thesis, and **4D orthographic pose vector**.

Every character designed by this skill — whether hero, villain, sect elder, or enforcer, in either `editorial` or `glamour` — must meet a **high-aesthetic commercial standard** (aligned with top-tier 3DCG animation and Oriental haute couture). Never downgrade a fantasy or drama character into mundane peasant labour, ugly grime, or pedantic feudal bureaucracy.

---

## 1. Thin-Brief Compiler (Step 0)

When the user supplies a rich script with specific scenes and power details, extract facts directly and skip to §2.

When the user supplies a **thin brief** (e.g. "设计一个魔教护法" or "设计一个清冷仙尊"), **never fall back to generic page-game clichés, and never invent mundane manual labour**. Instead, construct three high-aesthetic fantasy anchors before touching any visual axis, and mark them as `[inferred setting]` in the delivery note:

| Step | Question to force | What it decides downstream | Bad (Cliché or Mundane Downgrade) | Good (High-Aesthetic Oriental Fantasy) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0A. Core Dao & Power Signature (功法与法则意象)** | What specific cultivation art, elemental law, or lethal authority does this person wield? | Motif seed, material light behaviour, iris optics, and colour spark | "He does evil magic" (vague) OR "He scrapes sulfur in a workshop" (mundane peasant downgrade) | "He practices the Abyssal Blood-Jade & Phantom Chime art — sealing enemies' meridians with resonant blackened-gold bone needles and crimson silk threads" |
| **0B. Charisma & Structural Lock (气场与高定廓形锁)** | How does their status and temperament project through a high-fashion silhouette, and what is banned? | `Invariant` and `Forbidden` in the IFV triple; waist/slit cut and shoulder architecture | "He wears a standard black robe" OR "He wears a dirty leather butcher apron" | "An ultra-slender, high-waisted obsidian velvet and sheer smoke-gauze long coat with a single-side armored乌金 shoulder mantle and a high thigh slit; forbidden bulky page-game spikes" |
| **0C. Transcendent Trace (宿命与超凡印记)** | Where does their cultivation cost or inner ruthlessness show as a striking, poetic visual detail? | The contradiction signal (§5), the §1B facial/skin trace, or the bottom-right macro micro-expression | "Mud on his boots and calluses" (ugly/mundane) | "His left hand's fingertips are gradually turning into translucent dark ink-jade from channeling abyssal qi, framed by articulated blackened-gold finger rings" |

## 2. The three questions

Every costume answers three questions, visibly at full-body distance, within a high-aesthetic visual language:

1. **What realm, power, or authority do they command?** Shown in silhouette elongation, luxury fabric hierarchy (matte velvet, sheer siren-gauze, brocade), and structural hardware.
2. **Where does their temperament sit between restraint and release?** One zone of razor-sharp discipline (a high-buttoned throat collar, a tightly corseted waist-girdle, a bound sword-wrist) contrasted against one zone of dangerous fluidity or allure (an asymmetrical collarbone slit, a cascading sheer train, a high leg-slit).
3. **What is their narrative or transcendent trace?** What mark has their Dao, bloodline, or recent battle left on them — shown in frost-rimmed hems, dark-gold ember singeing, a hairline curse-sigil, or a gold-repaired blade slash (never ugly dirt or sweat stains).

## 3. Position and the ten dimensions

Specify **Position** first: **age presentation, gender presentation, and realm/command authority**.

| Dimension | What to specify (derive, never copy) | High-aesthetic physical evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Position & Aura** | visual age band, gender presentation, realm/status authority, and charisma archetype | e.g., `mid-twenties visual presentation, slender masculine/androgynous frame, Abyssal Sect Left Enforcer commanding blood-seal punishments` |
| **2. Face bone (§1A)** | facial index, jaw/chin contour, cheekbone plane, eye spacing, mouth width, nose profile | derived inside the commercial beauty band via [02-face-derivation.md](02-face-derivation.md) §1A |
| **3. Face surface (§1B)** | iris gem-optics, eye-corner styling, lip finish, porcelain specular map, 0-or-1 Dao/fate mark | derived from gaze & Dao aura via [02-face-derivation.md](02-face-derivation.md) §1B |
| **4. Hair architecture** | mass & length, crown height, face-framing locks, strand clump physics, inner value streak, 1–4 headpieces | flowing waist-to-ankle tresses, high martial ponytail, or asymmetrical half-up crown |
| **5. Body & proportion** | head-count band (`8.0–8.5` default for fantasy/glamour; `7.5–8.0` for grounded/youth), high waist-cinch, leg-to-torso ratio | see [03-costume-and-craft.md](03-costume-and-craft.md) §2.1 |
| **6. Orthographic pose** | weight axis, shoulder-neck line, poised empty-hand gesture, chin & gaze | see §7 below; mandatory in blueprint point 1 and ledger axis 10 |
| **7. Transcendent / fate traces** | spiritual veins, elemental rime/embers, jade-transformed fingertips, gold-mended battle cut | visible, poetic, and high-aesthetic |
| **8. Haute-couture construction** | layered sheer vs matte fabrics, collar/slit geometry, waist corsetry/girdle, treated vs raw/dissolving edges | how the garment sculpts and elongates the body |
| **9. Worn ornaments** | blackened gold, cold silver, mutton-fat/ink jade × mechanical closure/drape job; zero held props | every node anchors a cape, cinches a waist, or frames the face |
| **10. Micro-expression** | bottom-right macro cell only: subtle muscle shift + gaze intensity | e.g., `lower lids narrow two millimetres, one mouth corner curves up in a cold half-smile, gaze locked` |

### Cast-wide charisma spectrum (differentiating a project without uglifying)

Across a multi-character project, never differentiate characters by making some ugly, dirty, or dumpy. Differentiate them across **five high-aesthetic bone & temperament spectrums**:

1. **清冷谪仙型 (Aloof Celestial)**: elongated `8.5-head`, narrow refined jaw (`1.50`), level lids, cool porcelain, austere vertical column or wide cascading sleeves.
2. **昳丽邪魅型 (Alluring Abyssal)**: `8.5-head`, sharp high cheekbones, feline positive canthal tilt (`+6° to +8°`), half-lidded dangerous gaze, asymmetric cutouts or high slits.
3. **威严霸主型 (Sovereign Imperious)**: `8.5-head`, broad structured shoulders, defined square-tapered jaw (`0.94`), direct downward-appraising gaze, monumental mantle or bone-sculpted armor.
4. **凌厉少年/飒爽侠客型 (Razor Youth / Martial)**: `7.5-to-8-head` lithe athletic frame, crisp brow-to-eye distance, high ponytail, high-waisted agile split-hem tailoring.
5. **温润世家/破碎病美型 (Gentle Noble / Fragile Ethereal)**: `8.0-head`, softer lower-lid fullness, slight head tilt, layered translucent mist-gauze and warm jade anchors.

## 5. The seven high-aesthetic contradiction directions

Specificity comes from dramatic tension. Every character carries **one named contradiction direction** and a **concrete visual signal** (usable in both `editorial` and `glamour`; two characters in one project may not share the same signal):

| Direction | Aesthetic Formula | Dramatic Read | High-Aesthetic Example Signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. 圣洁皮囊 × 杀伐凶器 (Sacred Form / Lethal Edge)** | ethereal, divine or saintly attire + one chillingly lethal structural weapon-ornament | an angel or immortal who kills without mercy | immaculate moon-white layered gauze paired with articulated blackened-silver claw guards on the left fingers |
| **2. 极致华贵 × 禁欲枷锁 (Opulent Luxe / Ascetic Lock)** | sumptuous imperial brocade and gem-work + a severe, throat-locking collar or binding chain | gilded prisoner of bloodline or self-imposed戒律 | heavy gold-woven night-plum damask locked at the throat and wrists by cold iron-jade suppression rings |
| **3. 慵懒散漫 × 绝对权柄 (Languid Ease / Sovereign Seal)** | uncinched, off-shoulder, or half-open drapery + the unmistakable insignia of supreme rank | someone so powerful they do not need to stand at attention | outer robe slipping off one shoulder over an open collarbone, anchored by the sect's supreme obsidian dragon-seal |
| **4. 无瑕神颜 × 宿命残痕 (Flawless Porcelain / Fated Fracture)** | BJD surface perfection + one poetic, tragic fracture from a heavenly tribulation or broken oath | beauty heightened by a single irreversible loss | flawless porcelain face and regal attire, but the halo-like jade crown is cleanly sheared in half and mended with gold wire |
| **5. 温雅书卷 × 诡谲魔道 (Gentle Scholar / Eldritch Dao)** | refined, scholarly or courtly silhouette + eerie, inhuman motif details on close inspection | the smiling strategist or polite monster | a serene celadon scholar's long robe whose tone-on-tone jacquard and silver chains are shaped like skeletal spine vertebrae |
| **6. 蝉翼轻纱 × 冷硬玄甲 (Gossamer Sheer / Cold Armour)** | weightless, translucent flowing gauze + razor-sharp, heavy metallic armour plates on one zone | untouchable speed meeting crushing force | sheer smoke-violet organza sleeves floating around a torso encased in a sculpted blackened-steel corset-cuirass |
| **7. 神魔异相 × 人间旧物 (Inhuman Divinity / Mortal Tether)** | transcendent non-human traits (snow-silver hair tips, glowing sigil, slit pupils) + one humble mortal keepsake | a god or demon still bound to one human memory | abyssal-black sovereign attire and molten-gold eyes, with a single faded red braided cord tied around the right pinky finger |

## 6. Design thesis

Write one sentence before the prompt linking the character's Dao/conflict to a **high-aesthetic silhouette and luxury material**:

Formula: **`[Role & Dao/Conflict] who [signature bearing/action] wears [high-fashion silhouette & luxury material] as [visual manifestation].`**

- Fails (mundane/ugly): `a demonic enforcer who works at a furnace wears a dirty leather apron.`
- Passes (high-aesthetic Oriental fantasy): `an abyssal-sect enforcer who seals apostates' meridians with phantom chimes: an asymmetric obsidian-velvet and smoke-gauze long coat locked at the throat by a blackened-gold bone-needle gorget.`

## 7. Orthographic pose vector (4D Charismatic Stance)

A character sheet requires **orthographic view alignment** (front, exact 90° side, 180° back on one shared ground line at identical scale). **It forbids a stiff, lifeless mannequin stance (`neutral balanced stance`).**

Specify all **four components** in blueprint point 1 to give the turnaround sheet immediate star charisma while keeping the torso front-facing and unoccluded:

| Component | High-Aesthetic Options (derive from temperament) | Orthographic constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Weight axis** | poised weight settled on the back leg with front foot lightly pointed / weight shifted into one hip in an elegant S-curve / tall unyielding stance with heels together / coiled martial balance across a shoulder-width stance | both feet stay on the shared ground contact baseline |
| **2. Shoulder-neck line** | aristocratic level shoulders with a long swan-like neck / one shoulder slightly relaxed and dropped 2cm in languid ease / squared commanding shoulders with chin drawn back | keep chest and front placket facing straight ahead in view 1 |
| **3. Empty-hand action** | one hand lightly resting two fingers on the waist-jade or belt clasp while the other drapes by the thigh / one hand raised near the collarbone lightly touching a hanging hair-lock or collar chain / both hands loosely tucked inside wide layered cuffs / one hand resting behind the lower back (负手而立) | **zero held props** ([../contract/gates.md](../contract/gates.md) §4); never block the central chest hero ornament |
| **4. Chin & gaze** | chin lifted 5° looking down through half-lowered lashes (睥睨/俯视) / chin level with a cold, unblinking direct gaze / head tilted 4° with a faint, dangerous half-smile looking slightly sidelong | keep head within 0–10° of frontal in view 1 so the 90° profile stays accurate |

**Batch rule ([../contract/gates.md](../contract/gates.md) §9, axis 10):** Any two characters in the same project must differ on **at least two of `weight axis · empty-hand action · chin & gaze`**.

## 9. Prompt-ready block scaffolds

### A. Invariant identity block (~65–75 words)

```text
[Visual age + gender presentation + realm/command read], [8-to-8.5-head or 7.5-to-8-head proportion] with [high waist-cut and shoulder-waist-hip line], [derived high-aesthetic bone consequences from 02-face-derivation.md §1A], large almond eyes with a defined upper lid line and a clear double-lid crease, [derived iris gem-colour/optics from §1B] with radial fibre, a darker limbal ring, a bright secondary reflection and a wet meniscus on the lower lid, [derived eye-corner styling], a minimal nose bridge with [derived refined tip], [derived mouth width and lip finish] with a visible cupid's bow, [0 or 1 derived Dao/fate mark — omit if 0], and smooth porcelain skin with a soft subsurface glow and [derived velvet-matte cheek / sharp specular break]. [Hair mass, hairline, parting, permanent colour/inner streaks].
```

### B. Look and evidence block (~80–110 words)

```text
Standing with [weight axis], [shoulder-neck line], [poised empty-hand gesture], [chin & gaze]. [Transcendent trace & contradiction signal from §5]. [Density 4-clause haute-couture construction: layers in order with matte-vs-sheer/value contrast and waist/shoulder structure; 60–90% edges in one luxury trim language + named raw/sheer edges; descending jeweller-grade nodes with closure/drape jobs; motif family at macro/mid/micro scales + open area]. [Material drape/light behaviour + one counter-temperature chromatic anchor].
```
